Moedim — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Moedim is built to be quiet in every sense, including this one.

What we collect

Nothing.

Moedim has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The app does not send your data anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it: Moedim runs entirely on your device and has no server.

Where your data lives

Your events and tasks are stored in a local database on your device. Your settings — category choices, notification times, display options — are stored locally on your device. The calendar content (the day-by-day dataset and the verse file) ships inside the app itself.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We could not recover it for you even if you asked, because we never had it.

Notifications

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push servers are involved. Every notification channel can be switched off in Settings at any time, including with the one-tap “Use quietly” switch.

Purchases

The optional Moedim Plus subscription is processed entirely by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Purchase entitlements are verified through RevenueCat, which processes a random app-generated identifier and your store receipt for the sole purpose of confirming the subscription; RevenueCat receives no personal details from us. Manage or cancel the subscription any time in your store account settings.

Requesting data deletion

The only Moedim data that exists off your device is the anonymous subscription record described above — a random app-generated identifier and the store receipt, held by our subscription processor (RevenueCat). To have it deleted, email thunderpedegd@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and we will remove the record within 30 days and confirm by reply. Everything else Moedim stores — your events, tasks, and settings — lives only on your device and is deleted the moment you uninstall the app. Nothing else is kept, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Moedim collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new text will ship with the app update and be posted at this same address, with the date above refreshed.

Contact

Questions are welcome: thunderpedegd@gmail.com
